Why H&B ?

Alexander von Humboldt and Aimé Bonpland were a German geographer and a French botanist. Together they embarked on one of the most ambitious scientific expeditions of the early 19th century to the Americas and pioneered environmental science as we know it today.

Their observations led them to develop a then-revolutionary vision of nature as an interconnected system. This idea laid the groundwork for modern ecology and transformed our understanding of the relationship between humans and the environment. Their work resonated far beyond their own time and influenced many generations of scientists, including Charles Darwin.

With German and French ties close to my heart, I feel a particular connection to Humboldt and Bonpland's story. Their journey and friendship are a reminder of what people can achieve when they pool their skills and share their knowledge across borders.
