My professional journey

I am Alexis Poupardin.

After several years working in European affairs and international cooperation, I decided to return to geography through the technical lens of geomatics.

Geomatics allows me to combine my curiosity, analytical thinking, and creativity in my everyday work.

Learning by doing is at the heart of my approach. I see every project as an opportunity to explore new tools, strengthen my skills, and tackle real-world challenges.

I also strongly believe in the value of open-source GIS, which made my career change possible and continues to shape the way I learn and work.
